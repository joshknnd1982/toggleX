This plugin toggles various things.
nvda+0 starts the process. After that, press:
d - toggle speech dictionary
h - toggle headings
i - toggle indents
m - toggle automatic language switching
r - toggle trust voice's language when processing characters and symbols
t - toggle tables
c - toggle table cell coordinates
l - toggle lists
p - toggle position information
u - toggle shortcut keys
o - toggle table and list header announcement
`-= -  switch to a saved synth slot
a - Save current synth
k - Toggle links announcement
z - Toggle all processing
